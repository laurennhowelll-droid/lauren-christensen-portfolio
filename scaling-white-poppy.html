Growth & Operational Strategy

Scaling White Poppy

Building the marketing, ecommerce, operational, and reporting infrastructure needed to support rapid company growth.

RoleDirector of Marketing & Operations
CompanyWhite Poppy Preservation
Timeline2024–Present
Primary impactApproximately 3× company growth supported

The challenge

What needed to change

White Poppy was growing quickly, but the systems behind the customer experience and production process needed to mature at the same pace. Marketing, order data, production, shipping, reporting, and customer service all depended on processes that were increasingly difficult to manage manually.

My role

What I owned

I worked across leadership, marketing, operations, customer service, production, finance, and external development partners. My role was not limited to identifying problems—I translated them into practical workflows, technical requirements, policies, reports, and implementation plans.

The approach

How I worked through it

01

Map the business

Documented how customers, orders, products, production stages, payments, shipping, and support issues moved through the company.

02

Prioritize bottlenecks

Focused on high-impact friction: duplicated work, incomplete visibility, inconsistent data, and processes that could not scale with order volume.

03

Build connected systems

Aligned Shopify, Airtable, n8n, Klaviyo, Stripe, shipping tools, and internal reporting around one operational lifecycle.

04

Measure and improve

Created leadership reporting to connect operational changes with refunds, customer outcomes, costs, and revenue recovery.

Results

What changed

Supported approximately 3× business growth
Improved cross-functional visibility
Standardized scalable workflows
Reduced dependence on manual processes
Created stronger decision support for leadership

Tools & capabilities

ShopifyAirtablen8nKlaviyoStripeGA4APIsExecutive reporting

What I learned

Scaling is rarely solved by adding one new tool. The biggest gains came from connecting strategy, process, data, ownership, and customer experience into one operating model.
