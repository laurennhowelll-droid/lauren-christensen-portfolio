Service Design & Operations

Customer Experience & Policy Optimization

Designing policies and recovery programs that balance customer trust, operational feasibility, and financial sustainability.

RoleOperations and policy strategist
FocusRefunds, warranties, insurance, and recovery
GoalProtect the customer relationship while reducing avoidable loss
MethodOperational data + service design

The challenge

What needed to change

Customer issues were not always caused by craftsmanship. Shipping timing, bouquet condition, order changes, cancellations, and unexpected damage required policies that were fair, clear, and financially responsible.

My role

What I owned

I analyzed issue patterns, estimated direct and avoided costs, helped develop warranty and refund structures, and created leadership recommendations for recovery offers and customer communication.

The approach

How I worked through it

01

Separate issue types

Distinguished craftsmanship issues from shipping, timing, customer decisions, and unavoidable flower-condition problems.

02

Measure recovery

Tracked when complimentary repurchases or alternative resolutions preserved revenue that otherwise would have been lost.

03

Design clear policies

Created staged refund, warranty, insurance, approval, and customer-responsibility frameworks.

04

Support the team

Translated policy into practical guidance that customer service and operations could apply consistently.

Results

What changed

Recovered revenue from likely cancellations
Created clearer internal decision frameworks
Improved consistency in customer resolutions
Balanced service recovery with cost control
Connected policy decisions to actual operational data

Tools & capabilities

Operational reportingPolicy writingCustomer journey mappingFinancial analysisAirtable

What I learned

The best customer policy is not automatically the most generous or restrictive. It is the one that sets clear expectations and gives the team a consistent path to a fair resolution.
