About

I work where marketing, operations, customer experience, and technology overlap.

My career began in marketing, but as the companies I supported grew, I became increasingly focused on the systems behind the work.

How orders move. How teams communicate. How customer information is stored. How performance is measured. Where repetitive work can be automated—and where a better process can improve both the employee and customer experience.

Today, I lead initiatives across marketing, ecommerce, operations, automation, reporting, and business systems. I’m known for learning new platforms quickly, identifying bottlenecks, and translating business needs into practical, scalable solutions.
