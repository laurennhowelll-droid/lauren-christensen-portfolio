Analytics & Decision Support

Executive Reporting & Policy Strategy

Turning operational data into leadership-ready recommendations covering refunds, cancellations, complimentary repurchases, insurance, warranties, and shipping.

RoleAnalyst & strategy lead
AudienceCompany leadership
InputsOperational and financial records
OutputRecommendations tied to business impact

The challenge

What needed to change

The company had substantial operational data, but important questions were difficult to answer: What was driving refunds? Were insurance products profitable? Which cancellations could be recovered? What policy changes could reduce loss while protecting the customer experience?

My role

What I owned

I cleaned and categorized operational records, compared time periods, calculated financial impact, identified patterns, and translated the findings into concise leadership reports and policy recommendations.

The approach

How I worked through it

01

Define the decision

Started with the business question leadership needed to answer rather than reporting every available metric.

02

Standardize categories

Grouped inconsistent operational notes into useful themes such as cancellations, shipping issues, design concerns, repairs, and repurchases.

03

Calculate impact

Connected counts with revenue, material cost, repair cost, recovery, and program profitability.

04

Recommend action

Used the analysis to support warranty, insurance, shipping, cancellation, and complimentary repurchase decisions.

Results

What changed

Identified revenue recovery opportunities
Measured insurance performance
Clarified cancellation and refund drivers
Supported warranty and policy development
Created leadership-ready narratives from messy source data

What I learned

A useful report does more than summarize what happened. It gives leaders enough context to choose what to do next.
