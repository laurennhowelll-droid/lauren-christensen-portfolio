Ecommerce & Digital Transformation

Shopify Migration & Ecommerce Modernization

Leading the transition from a legacy ecommerce platform to Shopify while aligning product architecture, customer experience, analytics, payments, and operations.

RoleProject and ecommerce lead
PlatformShopify
ScopeCustomer-facing and backend operations
PartnersInternal teams and external agencies

The challenge

What needed to change

The migration needed to improve the customer experience without breaking the operational systems behind a highly customized floral preservation process. Product options, event dates, shipping workflows, customer accounts, payments, analytics, and integrations all had to remain aligned.

My role

What I owned

I helped define requirements, organize product and variant architecture, coordinate vendors, configure the Shopify environment, troubleshoot theme and checkout behavior, and connect marketing and operational systems.

The approach

How I worked through it

01

Translate requirements

Converted business and customer needs into technical and design requirements for internal and external teams.

02

Rebuild the catalog

Structured products, pricing, options, shipping profiles, and merchandising for a complex made-to-order business.

03

Connect the stack

Aligned Shopify with Klaviyo, analytics, tax, payment, customer-account, and operational systems.

04

Test the journey

Reviewed the customer path from product discovery through checkout, fulfillment, support, and post-purchase communication.

Results

What changed

Modernized the ecommerce foundation
Improved control over merchandising and checkout
Connected marketing and operational tools
Created a more scalable platform for future development
Strengthened coordination between business and technical teams
